# Ending gag: the oasis (no narration)

After "Congratulations, you built a camel.":
1. Proud camel reaches a small sparkling oasis, dips its head to drink. Camera stays wide.
2. Huge cartoon SLURRRP: the pond drains like a bathtub in ~1 s; the camel's belly visibly swells.
3. Tiny "pop" as the last drop goes.
4. One little fish is left flopping on dry, cracked mud; it stops, looks up at the camera, blinks.
5. Hard cut.
